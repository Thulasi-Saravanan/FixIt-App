⚠️ Safety Information

FixIt provides general troubleshooting guidance for common technology problems.

If a device is smoking, producing a burning smell, overheating severely, or showing signs of electrical damage, stop using it and seek help from a qualified technician.

Do not open electrical devices unless you have the required knowledge and equipment.
