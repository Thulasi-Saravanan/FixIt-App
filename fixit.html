Laptop Overheating

Based on your responses, your laptop is likely experiencing thermal issues. This could be due to dust accumulation in cooling vents, a failing fan, or insufficient ventilation space. Overheating can cause performance degradation and potentially damage components if not addressed.

Likely Causes

01
Dust Accumulation
Dust buildup in cooling vents restricts airflow, causing the system to overheat.
02
Failing Cooling Fan
A malfunctioning fan cannot effectively dissipate heat from the processor.
03
Poor Ventilation
Using the laptop on soft surfaces blocks air vents and reduces cooling efficiency.

Recommended Next Steps

  • Clean the cooling vents with compressed air
  • Check fan operation using system monitoring tools
  • Ensure proper laptop ventilation on hard surfaces
  • If problem persists, contact professional technician
